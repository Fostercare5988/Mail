# Mail

A better mailbox for WoW 1.12.1. Needs [ClassicAPI](https://github.com/brues-code/ClassicAPI).

- **Open All** takes the money and items from every mail and deletes the mail it
  emptied. C.O.D. mail, GM mail and plain letters are left alone. Right-click a
  mail to take just that one.
- **Send many items at once**: right-click bag items at the mailbox (right-click
  again to take one off), or drop them on the letter. Each item goes as its own
  mail, up to 21. Items waiting to go look locked in your bags, and you can
  still move or sort your bags.
- **Names complete** as you type: the people you mailed first, then your other
  characters, group, guild, friends and whispers. Tab picks the next name.
- The inbox shows stack counts, returned and auction-house mail, and the page.

No settings and no chat command: it just works at the mailbox.

## Install

Download `Mail.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/Fostercare5988/Mail/releases/latest)
and extract it into `Interface\AddOns`.
